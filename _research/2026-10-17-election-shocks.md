title: "The Industrial Effects of Election Shocks"
collection: research
category: work_in_progress   # working_papers | publications | work_in_progress
date: 2026-10-17
abstract: 'This paper investigates the disparate industrial effects of U.S. presidential election news and outcomes. By orthogonalizing prediction market probabilities to macroeconomic and financial data, I isolate unexpected shifts in electoral prospects. I then use these exogenous shocks to evaluate industries categorized by their political contributions, proxying for their alignment with a particular party''s policies. On impact, an unexpected increase in Republican win probabilities sharply raises stock prices for Republican-favoring industries, with little effect on Democrat-favoring counterparts. Conversely, medium-term responses reveal aggregate stock price declines alongside substantial decreases in employment and revenues for Democrat-favoring sectors. Ultimately, election shocks operate as news shocks, driving immediate and persistent sectoral reallocations.'
